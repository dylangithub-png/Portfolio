Welcome to the visual and artistic universe of **Delphine Moyard** and **Dylan Peruffo**.

We are two visual artists and photographers driven by the same creative dedication and a shared passion for light, texture, and visual storytelling. While we join forces during **duo photo shoots**, each of us also develops their own distinctive and complementary artistic journey.

---

### A Shared Vision: Duo Shootings

Working together allows us to bring a dual perspective to every photo session:
- **Crossed Gazes**: Two complementary framings, an ongoing dialogue to capture the decisive moment and subtle nuances.
- **Collaborative Art Direction**: Scene composition, natural light sculpting, and intimate atmospheres.
- **Versatility**: Fine art projects, intimate portraits, editorial commissions, and event reportage.

---

### Delphine Moyard — Photography & Paintings

A photographer with a refined and poetic sensibility, **Delphine Moyard** explores intimate portraiture, subtle shadow plays, and delicate textures.

Alongside photography, Delphine devotes herself to **fine art painting and canvas creations**:
- **Texture Explorations**: Working with pigments, coatings, raw canvas, and mixed media.
- **Dialogue Between Painting & Photography**: Exploring the tactile materiality of paint echoing the fleeting ephemerality of photographic light.
- **Original Artworks**: Pieces exhibited in galleries and bespoke commissions for private collectors and architect spaces.

---

### Dylan Peruffo — Photography, 3D Design & Workshops

**Dylan Peruffo** approaches photography with an eye drawn to vast landscapes, geometric lines, and travel documentary.

Beyond the photographic medium, Dylan expands his creativity into three-dimensional volume, digital fabrication, and education:
- **3D Design & Additive Manufacturing**: Designing contemporary art objects, sculptural luminaires, and bespoke interior pieces made with 3D printing technology.
- **Workshops & Education**: Mentoring photographers through hands-on photography workshops (shooting techniques, lighting, digital post-processing) and introduction sessions to 3D design and additive fabrication.
